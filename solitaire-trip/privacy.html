Privacy Policy — Solitaire Trip: Card Travel

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Solitaire Trip: Card Travel (package com.tlabgames.solitaire, the “Game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Viktor Trukhnov (VibesinGames) (“we”, “us”). Contact: v.trukhnov@gmail.com.

1. Information we do not collect

The Game has no accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or any other content on your device. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

2. Information collected automatically

DataCollected byPurpose
Game interaction events (levels started and finished, purchases made in the Game, ad views), app version, device model, OS version, country derived from IP, app-instance identifier Google Firebase AnalyticsUnderstanding how the Game is played, balancing difficulty, fixing problems
Advertising ID (Android), IP address, device and network information, ad interactions Google AdMobShowing ads, measuring them, preventing fraud; personalised ads only where you consent
Purchase details (product, order status) Google Play BillingProcessing in-app purchases. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see your card.

Data is sent over encrypted connections. We do not sell your personal information. We do not knowingly share it except with the service providers above, who process it under their own privacy policies: Firebase, Google AdMob, Google.

3. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded ads and ads between levels from level 6. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent on first launch; you can change your choice later in the Game settings. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Buying “No Ads” removes ads between levels; optional rewarded ads stay available.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and then deleted or aggregated. Progress on your device is removed when you uninstall the Game. To request deletion of analytics data, email us; because we hold no account data, we will explain how to identify your installation.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal data and to object to personalised advertising. Contact us to exercise them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
